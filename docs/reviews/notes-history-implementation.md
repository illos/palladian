# Note history (port plan A4)

Date: 2026-10-22; review rounds 1–5 on 2026-10-23. Branch `feat/notes-history`, on `main` at `0042ef8`, which contains A2 (note actions), A6 (pins and sort orders) and A3 (editor interactions), all merged and released. History joins A2's action registry. Owner request: the next step of the Deltos feature port ([plan 08](../specs/notes-app/08-deltos-feature-port.md), A4).

No backend change, and no change to the fundamentals. History uses the existing `history.list` and `history.read`, which already require edit access, `notes.content` for the note as it is now, and A2's Duplicate path (`notes.create`) for **Restore as a new note**. In-place Restore (`history.restore`) was built through review round 4 and withdrawn after round 5 (Design change, below); with it went its backend precondition and its `NoteChannel` getter, so `history.ts` and `channel.ts` are as on `main`. `cache.ts`, `live.ts`, `channel.ts`, `binding.ts`, `session.ts`, `logout.ts`, `main.tsx` and `editor.tsx` are unchanged, as are `selectNote`, `makeChannel`, `accessLost` and `openRemote`.

Differences from plan 08 A4, deliberately:

- **Restore as a new note, not in place.** Plan A4 restores a version over the note with `history.restore`. That was built and reviewed through four rounds, then withdrawn after round 5 at the owner's coordinator's direction (Design change, below).
- **Word diff, not character diff.** The plan calls Deltos's diff a character diff, but `lib/textDiff.ts` compares words and whitespace; Deltos's only character-level code is `computeCharDelta`, a "+120 −18" counter stored with its per-device snapshots. The word diff is ported. The counter is not: Palladian's list would need every version's text to compute it.
- **Deltos's 2,000-word cut is not ported.** Deltos truncated both texts at 2,000 tokens and silently dropped the rest from the comparison. The bounded fallbacks above replace it.
- **A "Version" view beside the two comparisons.** Deltos showed only the diff. The plan asks for the version rendered read-only and a diff toggle, so the toggle has three positions: Version, vs Previous, vs Current (Deltos's labels).
- **"Previous" is the version's own starting revision**, not the row below it in the loaded list, so it is exact on page boundaries and for versions that interleave with other people's.
- **Inline confirmation** instead of Deltos's separate confirm screen or a browser `confirm()`, so the version stays visible while confirming.
- **Styles in `history.css`**, loaded with the panel like `appearance.css`, rather than in `styles.css`. It follows `styles.css`'s variables and patterns and stays out of the A3 and A6 branches' changes to `styles.css`.

## Design change after review round 5

**In-place Restore is withdrawn; History restores a version as a new note instead.**

Why: `history.restore` replaces the whole note in one step. Typing on any device or tab that has not reached the server when that step arrives is rebased away: it disappears from the editor and its local journal, and was never on the server, so it is not in History either. That is collaboration behaviour in the fundamentals (`channel.ts`, the cache's notifications and prosemirror-collab rebasing), recorded below under Found and raised with the owner. Rounds 1–4 contained it from the app side, step by step: this window's own unsent typing, a hold on the note while a restore was in flight, durable records reconciled across reloads and windows, holds until each editor had received the restore, and an account precondition on the server. Round 5 (`notes-history-round5-astra-review.md`) found further gaps in that containment (time-bounded holds, storage loss, cross-window refusals, non-atomic expiry, a delayed sign-out, connection recovery). The conclusion: in-place Restore cannot be made safe in app code alone, and the fundamentals fix is not approved. So, until it exists, A4 ships Restore as a new note, which replaces nothing and so cannot drop anyone's typing.

Removed with it: the restore keeper and its IndexedDB store, the sender and reconciliation, the editing holds and "Restoring a version…" states, the 20-second close logic, the sign-out settlement, the `account` argument on `history.restore` and its backend tests (A4 now makes no backend change), the `NoteChannel.collabVersion` getter (A4 now leaves `channel.ts` untouched), and with them the proposed D28 exception for restore records, which no longer exist. Kept: the History action rules and its connection rule, browsing, comparing, paging and the read-only rendering, and the e2e helpers `dev-target.mjs` and `redact.mjs` with their tests.

What remains of in-place restore: agents can still restore in place through MCP `restore_revision`, which calls `history.restore`, and edit whole notes with `edit_note` `replace_all`; both still drop unsent typing on open devices (Found). In-place Restore can return to the app once the fundamentals fix exists; the round 1–4 work and reviews below record what it needed.

The round 1–4 responses below describe in-place Restore as it was. Of them, these remain in the code: F2 (History needs the connection), F3 (the close rule uses A2's targets), F4 (the page chain), R2-2 and R3-2 (`dev-target.mjs`), R2-3 and R3-3 (`redact.mjs`), and R3-4 (the actions e2e menu).

## Found during this work (not changed)

- **A whole-note replacement discards unsent typing on every other device and tab** (the reason in-place Restore was withdrawn). `history.restore` commits one step that replaces the whole document, and so does an agent's `edit_note` with `replace_all` (`notes-core` `edits.ts`, `replaceRange(tr, 0, doc.content.size, …)`); MCP `restore_revision` calls `history.restore`, and `replace_block` or `delete_block` do the same within one block. A device or tab with typing not yet confirmed when such a step arrives rebases its unconfirmed steps over it; those inside the replaced range map to nothing and are dropped, from the editor and its journal, and they were never on the server, so they are not in History either. Until round 4 the e2e showed this deterministically with in-place Restore (a held, unsent word gone and in no revision). This is collaboration behaviour in the fundamentals (`channel.ts`, the cache's notifications and prosemirror-collab rebasing), raised with the owner. The app's History no longer replaces anything; agents still can.
- **Downgrading an open note's editor to read closes the note for them.** The editor's step subscription is refused, the channel reports access lost, and `accessLost` closes the note with "You no longer have access to that note." and revokes its cached copy, although the person may still read it; the note stays in their list as shared with them. This is channel and `accessLost` behaviour (fundamentals). The e2e's downgrade step checks only that History closes and the server refuses it.
- **The existing `Modal` in `panels.tsx` closes when its padding is clicked.** It treats any click whose target is the `<dialog>` as a backdrop click, and its padding belongs to the dialog. The History dialog has no padding to avoid this; the other dialogs are unchanged.
- **`PanelBoundary` draws its failure box outside the dialog.** If a History query fails because access just dropped, the box can show for a moment before the panel closes.
- **`live-e2e.mjs`'s `typeAtEnd` helper can type at the start of the note.** It sets the DOM selection with a `Range` and types at once; in one of this branch's e2e runs, before the editor had taken up that selection, " Cy was here." landed before "Recipe", so the title changed and the History dialog's name with it. `history-e2e.mjs` now clicks into the last block, presses Ctrl+End and waits a frame, and checks the title after the setup edits. `live-e2e.mjs` is unchanged.
- (Resolved by A2 as merged: before the rebase, an open note kept the role it opened with, and its journaled row counted as a local draft with no actions. A2's `openTarget` and `isLocalNoteId` now handle both.)
